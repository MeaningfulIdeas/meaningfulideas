Our Approach

A Radical Approach to Parenting

Non-Coercive, Collaborative Parenting is a radical approach where we treat children with profound respect for their humanity.

💚

Honor Consent & Autonomy

We avoid mainstream authoritative tactics such as rules, consequences, limits, and boundaries — and instead honor our children's consent and autonomy.

🤝

Work Alongside Our Children

We foster partnership rather than imposing control, working together to meet everyone's needs.

🔄

Break Generational Patterns

Our goal is to break generational patterns and create safe, trustable, and loving relationships with our children that last throughout our lives.

Benefits

The Benefits of Collaborative Parenting

Shifting towards a collaborative approach brings profound changes to your family life.

🔒

Unshakable Trust

Build a foundation of trust that transforms power struggles into collaborative problem-solving, creating a more peaceful and harmonious home environment.

🧠

Emotional Intelligence

Help your kids develop emotional intelligence and healthy communication skills that serve them for life.

💪

Enhanced Self-Esteem

Watch your children flourish with enhanced self-esteem and a strong sense of their own worth and autonomy.

🌱

Long-Term Connection

Create a relationship of connection and trust that lasts throughout your lives — through childhood, teens, and beyond.

Personal Growth

A Transformative Journey for Parents Too

Non-Coercive, Collaborative Parenting isn't just about raising children — it's a transformative journey of self-discovery and healing for parents as well.

💗

Healing for Parents

We grow alongside our children, healing our own patterns and creating a legacy of connection, love, and mutual respect.
