Why This Course

When Trust Is Damaged, Kids Resist More

When we treat our kids in ways that make them trust our guidance less, they resist us more. When this happens, we're often at a loss for what to do — and end up resorting to coercion.

🔑

Build Trust

Create a relationship with so much safety that your children trust your guidance naturally.

💬

Increase Cooperation

When kids feel their needs are important and taken seriously, they're much more likely to work with us.

Practical Strategies

A series of strategies you can implement immediately to create a deeper guiding relationship with your kids.
